# POL-008: Acceptable Use and Data Handling Policy

**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-05
**Owner:** IT Service Management (in coordination with Legal and HR)
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and interns

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines boundaries on what the IT Help Desk (including the AI Help Desk Agent) may do with company data and equipment, and identifies categories of requests that fall outside standard IT ticket handling and must be redirected.

## 2. Data Handling Boundaries for Automated Resolution

The AI Help Desk Agent must never, under any circumstances:

- Disclose another employee's personal data (contact details, HR records, performance information) in the course of resolving a ticket, even if the requester claims a legitimate need — such requests must be redirected to HR.
- Grant itself or a requester access to a system by bypassing the approval chain defined in POL-002.
- Share credentials, temporary passwords, or MFA backup codes through an unverified or unencrypted channel.
- Make any final decision on suspected employee misconduct, harassment, or policy violation reported through a ticket — these must be redirected to HR or Legal immediately without the AI attempting to investigate or resolve the underlying complaint.

## 3. Out-of-Scope Requests

The following request types are outside the scope of standard IT ticket handling and must be redirected rather than resolved:

- Complaints about a colleague's conduct, harassment reports, or workplace disputes → redirect to HR.
- Requests involving legal holds, litigation, or contract disputes → redirect to Legal.
- Requests for exceptions to company policy (e.g., "can I skip the approval process") → redirect to the requester's manager or the relevant policy owner; the Help Desk does not have authority to grant policy exceptions.
- Payroll, benefits, or compensation questions → redirect to HR.

## 4. Acceptable Use of Equipment

Standard IT support assumes company equipment is used primarily for business purposes. Reports of a device malfunction are handled under POL-003 regardless of what the employee was doing on the device at the time, unless the ticket itself reveals a suspected policy violation (e.g., unauthorized software, prohibited content), in which case the malfunction may still be resolved but the policy concern should be separately flagged for manager/HR awareness rather than acted upon by the Help Desk.

## 5. Escalation Criteria

- Any ticket that, on its face or through the conversation, reveals a report of harassment, misconduct, or a workplace dispute — escalate immediately and redirect to HR; do not attempt further troubleshooting-style resolution of the underlying complaint.
- Any request asking the Help Desk to bypass an approval requirement defined in another policy.
- Any request for another employee's personal or HR data.
