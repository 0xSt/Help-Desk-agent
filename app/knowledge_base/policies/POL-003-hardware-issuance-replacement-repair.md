# POL-003: Hardware Issuance, Replacement and Repair Policy

**Version:** 1.9
**Effective Date:** 2025-03-10
**Owner:** IT Service Management
**Applies To:** All employees issued company hardware

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines standard procedures for diagnosing hardware malfunctions, issuing loaner equipment, approving replacements, and handling repair requests for laptops, desktops, and peripherals.

## 2. Standard Diagnostic Procedure

Before recommending replacement or repair, the following basic diagnostics should be attempted where applicable:

1. Power cycle / hard reset.
2. Cable, port, and peripheral swap testing to isolate the faulty component.
3. Driver update check for display, network, and peripheral issues.
4. Remote inspection of running processes for performance issues (overheating, slowness).

## 3. Loaner Equipment

A loaner device may be issued **immediately, without prior approval**, when:

- A primary work device is completely non-functional (will not power on, critical hardware failure), **and**
- The employee has an active business need that cannot wait for standard repair turnaround.

Loaner issuance should be logged against the employee's asset record and the original device scheduled for depot repair in parallel.

## 4. Liquid Damage and Physical Damage

Any device with suspected liquid or physical damage must be powered down immediately and not restarted, to avoid further internal damage. These cases go directly to depot repair; no remote troubleshooting should be attempted for keyboard, trackpad, or internal component symptoms following a spill or drop.

## 5. Hardware Replacement Approval Thresholds

Hardware refresh and replacement requests follow these approval rules:

| Scenario | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Device is beyond standard 4-year refresh cycle and is demonstrably failing | Manager approval |
| Replacement requested for a device under 4 years old, still functional | Manager **and** IT asset owner approval |
| Standard peripheral request within existing inventory (mouse, keyboard, docking station, monitor) at standard allowance | No approval required |
| Non-standard or premium equipment request (e.g., additional monitors beyond standard allowance, specialized peripherals) | Manager approval |

Any hardware request exceeding these thresholds without documented approval must be escalated to a human agent rather than auto-approved.

## 6. Depot Repair

Devices requiring internal repair (motherboard, display panel, keyboard assembly, battery replacement) are sent to depot repair. Standard depot turnaround is 3–5 business days. A loaner should be issued for the duration whenever the employee has an active business need.

## 7. Escalation Criteria

- Replacement or repair request exceeding the approval thresholds in Section 5 without documented approval.
- Repeated hardware failures on the same asset within 90 days (may indicate a defective unit warranting different handling than routine repair).
- Any request involving executive-level equipment (premium/high-spec devices follow a separate procurement track).
